Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Labra handles user data. Labra is intended for personal organization of lab results, medical values, notes, and files.

1. Data the app may store

Depending on what you enter or attach, Labra may store:

  • profiles for people or pets;
  • analysis names, dates, laboratories, and notes;
  • medical values, units, and reference ranges;
  • attached files, including PDFs or images of results;
  • app settings and local operational data.

2. Where data is stored

Labra's current model is centered on local storage on the user's device. If a future version adds sync, cloud backup, or accounts, this policy should be updated before those features are launched.

3. Sharing with third parties

Labra does not sell personal or medical user data. The app should not share your local medical records with third parties unless you take a separate action or explicitly enable a feature that does so.

4. Files, recognition, and imports

If the app analyzes attachments or recognizes text from files, that processing is intended to create or fill your records. Always review recognized values manually because automatic recognition may be inaccurate.

5. Backups and deletion

You are responsible for backing up your device data. Deleting the app, clearing its data, or resetting the device may permanently remove local records and files.

6. Security

Medical data is sensitive. Protect your device with a password, biometrics, or other operating-system security features. Do not enter data you do not want stored on this device.

7. Children and family profiles

If you store data for a child, family member, or pet, make sure you have the right to keep those records and show them to medical professionals.

8. Policy changes

This policy may be updated when app features, data storage behavior, or legal requirements change. The new effective date will be shown on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, use the project repository on GitHub: Aecttann/labra-site.
