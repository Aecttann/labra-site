Privacy Policy

How the current Android version of Labra handles data.

At a glance

Medical records stay local. OCR runs on-device. Google ML Kit may send technical telemetry. Android backup and synchronization are not available.

01Who we are and what this covers

Labra is developed by aectann. Support and privacy contact: aesonne101@gmail.com. This policy describes the current Android app and this website. The app is intended for adults organizing laboratory records for themselves, family members and animals.

02Records you add

Your device stores the profiles you create: name, type, optional date of birth and photo, and species and breed for animals. Records may contain report titles, dates, laboratories, notes, values, units, reference intervals, PDFs and images. Preferences such as your selected theme are also stored. These records support display, search, editing and result history.

03Local storage and recognition

Medical records stay on your device. Labra does not upload them to its own server or sell them. No account is required. PDF and image content is processed on-device using Tesseract, PDFBox and Google ML Kit. ML Kit does not send input images or recognized text to Google. Review recognized data against the original report before saving.

04Google ML Kit technical data

Google ML Kit may send technical information to Google, including device manufacturer and model, OS version, app package and version, a per-installation SDK identifier, performance, OCR configuration, input and output sizes, API events and error codes. Google describes using these metrics for diagnostics, analytics, API maintenance and improvement, and abuse detection. Documented telemetry is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. The current interface has no switch to opt out of this automatic SDK telemetry. These are technical metadata rather than medical-report content. See ML Kit Terms and Privacy, its Android data disclosure and Google’s Privacy Policy. Google’s practices govern retention and rights for data it has already received. Local deletion in Labra does not promise deletion of that data.

05Files, camera and other apps

You select documents through the system file picker and profile photos through the system photo picker. To photograph a report, Labra opens an external camera app. This flow does not require access to your entire photo library. A document provider may show files from your cloud storage; its own policy governs that service. If you open an attachment in another app, your action grants that app access to a decrypted copy, which it handles under its own rules.

06Security and temporary copies

The persistent Android database uses SQLCipher. Managed attachments and avatars use AES-GCM encryption, with the root key protected by Android Keystore. OCR, camera capture and previews may create temporary decrypted data in memory or private cache. We therefore do not claim that every temporary copy is always encrypted. Protect your device with a screen lock. Encryption does not remove the risks of an unlocked or compromised device.

07Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you delete them or clear app data. You can delete individual reports, profiles or attachments, or all local data in Labra Settings. Deleting managed records does not delete originals in your gallery, Downloads, camera, cloud provider or copies received by external viewers. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes its local storage; it does not delete data previously sent to third-party services. The developer cannot remotely access local records to recover or delete them.

08Backups and device loss

The current Android app has no backup export/import or synchronization. Android backup and transfer of managed data are disabled through the app’s system rules. Do not rely on automatic recovery after reinstalling, replacing or losing a phone. Keep original medical and veterinary reports separately.

09Family and animal records

Only add another person’s information when you have the appropriate permission or authority. The app is not intended for children to use independently. Adults may manage records for children in their care. Keep veterinary results in the relevant animal’s profile; human reference intervals are not universal animal intervals.

10This website and support messages

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. This site has no advertising, analytics integrations or tracking scripts added by us. Fonts and images are served with the site. GitHub may process IP addresses and technical request logs as the hosting provider: GitHub’s privacy statement. External sites have their own policies. If you email support, the developer receives your email address and the content you choose to send through Gmail. Avoid sending medical reports or other people’s information unnecessarily. Messages are retained to address the request and related obligations. Contact aesonne101@gmail.com to request access or deletion.

11Questions, rights and changes

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights of access, correction, deletion or restriction of processing. Use in-app controls for local records; contact aesonne101@gmail.com regarding information sent to the developer. You may also contact a competent data-protection authority where applicable. This policy will be updated when actual practices change, with the version date shown above. Mentioning future changes does not itself authorize new processing.
