01Purpose

Labra is a tool for personal storage and organization of laboratory records for people and animals. Follow these terms and applicable law when using it. Stop using the app if you do not agree. Developed by aectann; contact aesonne101@gmail.com.

02Limits of medical use

Labra is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, or a veterinarian for animal care. Do not use the app as a basis for changing treatment, taking medication or making emergency decisions. Contact appropriate emergency services when urgent care is needed.

03Reviewing results

Check entered and recognized data against the original: profile, date, indicator name, comparison signs, decimal separator, units and reference intervals. A color label or chart does not establish a healthy or pathological state. Use veterinary laboratory intervals appropriate to the animal species and test.

04Authority to keep records

Only add data and files you have the right to store. You need the appropriate permission or authority to keep records for other people, including children. The product is intended for adult users.

05Keeping originals

Keep original copies of important reports separately. Android backup and synchronization are not currently available. Uninstalling, clearing data, or losing the device or encryption key may permanently remove local records. Do not rely on recovery by the developer.
