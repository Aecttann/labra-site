What Labra does

The app is built to organize medical results, not to diagnose conditions. It helps you see history and find the right data faster.

01

Analysis catalog

Fast search and suggestions for common laboratory tests.

02

Reference ranges

Save values with units, minimum and maximum ranges, and notes.

03

Trends

History helps compare results over time and prepare for medical appointments.

04

Attachments

Keep result files next to the related analysis instead of searching across folders.

05

Reminders

Repeat tests are easier to plan when previous dates and context are already saved.

06

Local control

Labra is designed around personal control over medical records on the device.

Privacy by default

Medical data is sensitive, so the site and policy describe the app plainly: user data should remain under user control.

No account required

Unless a separate sync feature is enabled in your app version, Labra does not require an account.

Local storage

Profiles, analyses, values, and attachments are stored on the user's device.

Not medical advice

Labra helps organize information, but health decisions should be made with a medical professional.

Important

Labra does not replace a doctor, laboratory, or medical information system. Even if the app includes tips, explanations, or suggestions, they are informational only and cannot replace consultation with a real doctor. Reference ranges depend on the laboratory, method, age, health condition, and other factors.
